RankedTools 0.1.31 is an experimental release that adds ranked OneSaber recommendations and includes the BeatLeader modifier work developed since 0.1.28.

- **OneSaber:** BeatLeader PP Gain, Not Played, To Improve, and both Maps to Conquer modes include ranked OneSaber charts automatically. Standard and OneSaber use one accuracy fit and clearability model. Song details identifies the recommended OneSaber difficulty.
- **BeatLeader Modifiers:** Enable this setting to compare ranked speed and compatible scoring modifiers in personal and clan playlists. It defaults to off. Song details shows the recommended modifiers and effective stars. Personalized clan capture targets and legacy star caps use the selected modifier option.
- **Attempt history:** Local and imported BeatLeader records now coalesce when they represent the same play but their timing fields differ.

Close Beat Saber before updating. On PC, copy all contents of the matching ZIP into the folder containing `Beat Saber.exe`. Refresh RankedTools after updating, then reload the generated playlists in your playlist browser. See the [PCVR installation guide](https://github.com/Kaiiak04/RankedTools/blob/v0.1.31/pcvr/README.md) and [tester checklist](https://github.com/Kaiiak04/RankedTools/blob/v0.1.31/pcvr/TESTING.md).

The earlier Quest modifier build was confirmed in game. Opening generated OneSaber recommendations and testing the Windows packages in game are pending. `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers all three installable assets, and `BUILD-VALIDATION.json` records their source and automated checks.
