# RankedTools 0.1.24

Clan playlists now recommend the additional contribution your next play could make, using your current skill, eligible PBs and the clan's weighted scores. Estimated captures come first, followed by useful progress toward conquest and weighted clan PP gain. New charts and replays compete together, including maps the clan has never played. Modifier PBs remain part of the baseline, and existing contributions are replaced rather than counted twice.

The search remembers useful recommendations and verified impact summaries. Previous picks receive fresh checks within the existing verification budget, while new discoveries compete under the same final ranking. Rotating exploration no longer removes a good recommendation simply because it was not checked again. Negligible contributions stop repeatedly taking the best-candidate slots. Secondary clans remain explicitly labeled **what-if primary clan**; personalized estimates transfer the existing PB before measuring further improvement.

Refreshes use 500-row bulk downloads, short-lived persistent screening caches, indexed PB lookups, and at most two verification jobs with shared pacing. Final ratings, rivals and contribution lists stay fresh. Essential-input failures preserve prior playlists, individual verification failures report reduced coverage, and rate limiting stops queued requests. This remains an approximate search with a bounded per-clan verification budget.

The optional **Legacy Clan Playlists** toggle restores closest-clan-gap ordering in the same playlist files. It has no minimum star cutoff or manual star controls. Its automatic upper cap now comes directly from the clearability model at **Min Clear Rate**, correcting the inherited personal-PP target plus 1.5-star padding. At 0%, the upper cap is disabled. Eligible PBs at or above predicted unmodified PP are omitted, including higher modifier PBs, leaving room for unplayed charts and predicted improvements. Legacy mode leaves personalized search history intact.

Clan previews explain current/predicted personal PP, clearability, supporting history, weighted contribution, remaining gap, capture targets and search coverage. Legacy previews display their automatic cap and current clan gap. Playlist filenames, artwork, track limits, and main/all-clan controls are preserved.

Targets Quest Beat Saber **1.40.8_7379**, with the existing Scotland2 and mod dependencies. This is the final planned update for this target before migration to **1.45.3_30054** alongside compatible PlaylistManager, BeatLeader and ScoreSaber dependencies. The QMOD is not packaged for the newer game build.

Validation passed 3,716 recommendation/API checks, 55 playlist-writer checks and 16 HTTPS-client checks (3,787 total), the Quest arm64 build, manifest schema and package-content checks. The packaged binary matches the build, with the existing source-built curl/Mbed TLS stack, bundled CA file, artwork and license notices. Accuracy and clearability remain heuristic estimates, and capture-at-prediction is not a calibrated probability. More detail is recorded in [clan recommendation details](docs/CLAN_RECOMMENDATIONS.md); the final cap change still requires in-game testing.
